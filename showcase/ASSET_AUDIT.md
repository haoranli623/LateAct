## Canonical visual selection

The showcase uses validated Gate 1 scene `0005`, direction `right_to_left`, with action arrival after two OLD-action NFEs. It was selected by a defensible representativeness rule rather than maximum effect: direct response `0.19148850302949214` is close to the 16-pair median `0.21823602063469516`; minimal-rollback response `0.996577090493037` is close to the cohort median `0.997053723726691`; future SSIM to NEW is `0.9209651572282391`; both decode and state audits pass. Visual inspection then confirmed a clean road scene, strong OLD/NEW yaw separation, LateAct/full-restart/NEW agreement, and no distracting rendering artifacts. Exact per-case provenance is in `canonical_example.json`.
